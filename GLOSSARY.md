# Wavelength XI

A football management game where synergy between players matters more than their individual ratings.

## Language

### Modes

**Draft**:
The single-season mode: the user builds a squad from historic player-seasons and plays one Premier League season in a chosen year, taking the place of a newly promoted side.
_Avoid_: Roulette (the handoff's earlier name for this mode)

**Dynasty**:
The multi-season mode: the user keeps managing one team across seasons, with generated players coming up through the lower leagues.
_Avoid_: Perpetual, career mode

### Players

**Player**:
A footballer's identity, which lasts across seasons.

**Player-season**:
One player as they were in one specific season, with that season's tendencies and ratings. Wayne Rooney 2010 and Wayne Rooney 2015 are two player-seasons of one player.
_Avoid_: Card, version
