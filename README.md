# Wavelength XI

> 🚧 Under construction. There's no code yet; the project is still in concept and planning.

A football management game where synergy between players matters more than their individual ratings. Advanced stats profile each player's playstyle, and how well those playstyles fit together earns bonuses for good partnerships and penalties for clashes.

- **Draft** (MVP): build a squad from historic player-seasons and play one Premier League season in a chosen year, taking the place of a newly promoted side.
- **Dynasty** (later): manage one team across seasons, with generated players coming up through the lower leagues.

The name comes from "on the same wavelength": two players who understand each other.

Planning lives in [issue #1](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/wavelength-xi/issues/1). Terms are defined in [`GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md).
