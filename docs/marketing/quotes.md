# Quotes: team over individual quality

Lines from famous managers and players that back up the game's premise: a team built to work together beats a group of individually stronger players. They are kept for possible marketing and branding use later, and are **not in use yet**.

Checked 2026-11-05. Each quote is kept in its original language, with an English translation. Translations are ours unless stated otherwise.

**Before using any of these:** use the verification status below. Using a famous person's name to promote a product also raises a right-of-publicity question, separate from whether the quote is accurate. Check that alongside the trademark search before going commercial (see `HANDOFF.md`).

## Arrigo Sacchi

Verified through named, dated broadcast interviews that news outlets reported.

> "Il calcio non è uno sport individuale, come tanti pensano."
>
> "Football is not an individual sport, as many think."

> "Non volevo individualisti, giocatori avidi, ma giocatori che giocassero con la squadra e per la squadra."
>
> "I didn't want individualists, greedy players, but players who played with the team and for the team."

Source for both: TMW Radio interview, reported by [FC Inter 1908](https://www.fcinter1908.it/ultimora/sacchi-maradona-calcio-sport/) (21 Oct 2022).

> "Il grande Milan, l'Ajax e il Barcellona di Guardiola sono state l'esaltazione del collettivo. [...] Il calcio è sempre stato intelligenza collettiva."
>
> "The great Milan, Ajax and Guardiola's Barcelona were the exaltation of the collective. [...] Football has always been collective intelligence."

Source: Eurosport interview, reported by [TuttoJuve](https://www.tuttojuve.com/altre-notizie/sacchi-calcio-intelligenza-collettiva-juve-rinnega-i-valori-della-vita-535473) (10 Nov 2020).

> "No era un equipo, era una película... pero faltaba la trama."
>
> "It wasn't a team, it was a film... but it lacked a plot."

Sacchi on Real Madrid's Galácticos.

Status: **only partly verified.** This comes from a Gazzetta dello Sport interview, as reported in Spanish by [Noticias Caracol](https://www.noticiascaracol.com/golcaracol/el-real-madrid-de-los-galacticos-no-era-un-equipo-era-una-pelicula-rg10). We haven't found the Italian original or its date, and the Spanish wording is already a translation. Find the Gazzetta article before using it.

## Johan Cruyff

Status: **not verified.** The line is widely attributed to Cruyff, but no book, column or interview has been found. Quote sites only copy each other. Check a Cruyff book (e.g. *Mijn verhaal* / *My Turn*, 2016) or the De Telegraaf archive before using it.

> "Als je voor elke positie de beste speler kiest, heb je nog geen sterk elftal maar een team dat als los zand uiteenvalt."
>
> "If you pick the best player for every position, you don't have a strong XI yet, but a team that falls apart like loose sand."

Seen on [Topquote](https://topquote.nl/quote/als-je-voor-elke-positie-de-beste-speler-kiest-heb-je-nog-geen-sterk-elftal-maar-een-team-dat-als-los-zand-uiteen-valt) and other quote sites.

The English line "Choose the best player for every position, and you'll end up not with a strong XI, but with 11 strong 1's" is **not** a translation of the line above. It reworks a Dutch pun also credited to Cruyff, with no source:

> "Eén goed elftal of elf goede ééntallen."
>
> "One good team of eleven, or eleven good teams of one."

Seen as the headline of a 2014 [ManagementSite](https://www.managementsite.nl/expeditie-duurzaam/wat-heb-jij-nu-liever-een-goed-elftal-of-elf-goede-eentallen-johan-cruijff) column. The column itself is the author's own essay.

## Alfredo Di Stéfano

Status: **not verified.** The line is widely attributed to him, but the earliest sources found are columns written after his death in July 2014, such as [Público](https://www.publico.es/opinion/hemeroteca/di-stefano-hay-mejor-jugador-todos.html), 8 Jul 2014. None of them say where he said it. [La Galerna](https://x.com/lagalerna_/status/2073352714373693641) says it hangs in the Bernabéu tunnel, but Real Madrid hasn't confirmed that. The best lead is his memoir *Gracias, vieja* (Aguilar, 2000).

> "Ningún jugador es tan bueno como todos juntos."
>
> "No player is as good as all of them together."

Until it's traced, credit it as "attributed to Alfredo Di Stéfano".
